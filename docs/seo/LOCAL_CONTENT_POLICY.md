# Local patient content and source policy

Recorded on 20 September 2026 from the clinic owner's publishing preference.

## Audience and usefulness

- Write for patients in Mangalagiri, Andhra Pradesh, India. Answer a specific patient question before adding search keywords.
- Use verified clinic services, doctors, contact details, landmarks and opening hours. Use Indian rupees for any confirmed prices; do not invent fees, insurance coverage, schemes or discounts.
- Include practical appointment questions and explain that treatment depends on individual assessment. Do not promise painless care, permanent results or a fixed number of visits for everyone.
- Do not create near-duplicate pages for surrounding towns or add local statistics without evidence.

## Choosing sources

1. Prefer relevant Indian primary sources: Indian dental professional bodies, government health resources and recognised Indian teaching institutions. Use Andhra Pradesh official sources for state-specific matters.
2. Read the linked page and check the exact claim. A respected organisation's homepage is not evidence for an unrelated treatment statement. Prefer a direct patient-information page.
3. Use an international source only when it adds a specific benefit that an appropriate Indian source does not provide. Explain its relevance to Indian patients. Do not add an overseas link merely for apparent authority.
4. Keep medical education separate from law and regulation. Never present a foreign regulator's approval, foreign insurance rules or overseas patient-rights guidance as applicable Indian or Andhra Pradesh requirements.
5. For claims about registration, consent, advertising, device approval, privacy, schemes or other rules, verify the current official Indian/Andhra Pradesh provision, effective date and scope before publishing. Do not turn general clinical guidance into a legal claim or imply that Tanvi has an unverified approval or affiliation.
6. Source links do not constitute review by Tanvi's doctors. Only add a clinical reviewer and date after actual review and approval.
